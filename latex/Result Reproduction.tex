the paper's GL coefficients and square-defect profiles.  The calculations use

\section{Li et al., ``Vortex dynamics of a d + is-wave superconductor''}

We mapped Li \textit{et al.}'s parameters and relaxation factors onto the
$s+d$ TDGL model.  The \texttt{quick} runs use a $13\times13$ structured grid,
$\Delta t\leq2\times10^{-3}$, and one flux quantum per magnetic-periodic cell.
Figure~3 includes $q=1$ and $q=10$ only.  Opposite sides now obey the paper's
magnetic translations, so there is no physical perimeter or $B=H$ boundary.
Remaining differences reflect short relaxations, coarse resolution, and
parameters omitted by the paper; many of our states are not converged well, but we still reproduce the results roughly.
